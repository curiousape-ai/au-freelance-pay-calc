Quick answer

For a freelancer invoicing $110,000 a year (GST exclusive), estimated FY2025-26 income tax for an Australian resident is $23,788, plus a Medicare levy of $2,200. Estimated take-home is $84,012 a year ($1,616 per week). At 220 billable days that is about $500.00 per day exclusive of GST ($550.00 including GST). This models contractor revenue, not a PAYG salary: employees also have tax withheld by their employer and super on top. The deductions field reduces taxable income; HELP and MLS are not modelled; super is shown as a separate set-aside. FAQ

  • How much tax does a freelancer pay on $110,000 a year in Australia (FY2025-26)?

    For $110,000 taxable income with no deductions, income tax is $23,788 plus Medicare $2,200 (combined $25,988, effective 23.6%).

  • What is take-home on $110,000 after tax and Medicare?

    Estimated take-home is $84,012 a year, or about $1,616 per week. Super, HELP/HECS, MLS and deductions are not modelled.

  • What contractor day rate equals $110,000 at 220 days?

    At 220 days, $110,000 GST exclusive is about $500.00/day ex GST, or $550.00 including GST.

  • Does this $110,000 figure include GST?

    This preset treats $110,000 as GST-exclusive annual contractor revenue. GST on the inc-GST equivalent would be $11,000. Employee salaries never include GST — leave the toggle on exclusive for a salary-like comparison.

  • Is the Medicare levy charged on $110,000?

    Yes in this estimate: above the FY2025-26 shade-out of $35,013, so Medicare is 2% = $2,200.
