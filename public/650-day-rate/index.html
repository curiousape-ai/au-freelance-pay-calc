Quick answer

A $650 GST-exclusive contractor day rate over 220 billable days is $143,000 a year before tax (about $157,300 including 10% GST). For an Australian resident in FY2025-26, estimated income tax is $34,248 and the Medicare levy is $2,860 (2% above the shade-out). That leaves take-home of about $105,892, or $2,036 per week. Super, HELP/HECS, MLS and deductions are not modelled. Confirm with the ATO or a registered tax agent — this is an estimate only, not advice.

FAQ

  • How much is a $650 day rate annually in Australia?

    At 220 billable days, $650 GST exclusive is $143,000 a year before tax, or $157,300 including 10% GST ($14,300 GST).

  • What income tax applies to a $650/day contractor rate (FY2025-26)?

    Treating $143,000 GST-exclusive revenue as taxable income with no deductions, resident income tax is about $34,248 using ATO FY2025-26 brackets.

  • What is estimated take-home on $650 per day?

    After $34,248 income tax and $2,860 Medicare levy, take-home is $105,892/year (about $2,036/week). Effective rate 25.9%.

  • Is the $650 day rate GST inclusive or exclusive on this page?
