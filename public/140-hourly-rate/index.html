FAQ

  • Which financial year does this use?

    Baked figures use FY2026-27. Use the financial-year switch to compare FY2025-26.

  • How is take-home calculated?

    GST-exclusive revenue minus deductions, resident income tax, Medicare and an optional HELP repayment. The default is 220 billable days.

  • Does this include super?

    For contractors, 12% is shown as a voluntary set-aside. For employees, 12% employer super is shown on top of salary.

  • Is this tax advice?

    No. It is an estimate for planning. Confirm your circumstances with the ATO or a registered tax agent.
