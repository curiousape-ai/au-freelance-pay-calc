Quick answer

For a freelancer invoicing $70,000 a year (GST exclusive), estimated FY2025-26 income tax for an Australian resident is $11,788, plus a Medicare levy of $1,400. Estimated take-home is $56,812 a year ($1,093 per week). At 220 billable days that is about $318.18 per day exclusive of GST ($350.00 including GST). This models contractor revenue, not a PAYG salary: employees also have tax withheld by their employer and super on top. No deductions, HELP, MLS or super are modelled. FAQ

  • How much tax does a freelancer pay on $70,000 a year in Australia (FY2025-26)?

    For $70,000 taxable income with no deductions, income tax is $11,788 plus Medicare $1,400 (combined $13,188, effective 18.8%).

  • What is take-home on $70,000 after tax and Medicare?

    Estimated take-home is $56,812 a year, or about $1,093 per week. Super, HELP/HECS, MLS and deductions are not modelled.

  • What contractor day rate equals $70,000 at 220 days?

    At 220 days, $70,000 GST exclusive is about $318.18/day ex GST, or $350.00 including GST.

  • Does this $70,000 figure include GST?

    This preset treats $70,000 as GST-exclusive annual contractor revenue. GST on the inc-GST equivalent would be $7,000. Employee salaries never include GST — leave the toggle on exclusive for a salary-like comparison.

  • Is the Medicare levy charged on $70,000?

    Yes in this estimate: above the FY2025-26 shade-out of $35,013, so Medicare is 2% = $1,400.
