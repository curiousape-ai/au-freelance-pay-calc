Quick answer (example)

Example (not a quote): a $800 GST-exclusive day rate over 220 billable days is $176,000 a year before tax. For an Australian resident in FY2025-26, estimated income tax is $46,458 and the Medicare levy is $3,520 (2% after the low-income shade). That leaves take-home of about $126,022, or $2,424 per week. Change the inputs for your own rate. Super, HELP/HECS, MLS and deductions are not modelled. This is an estimate only — confirm with the ATO or a registered tax agent.

Superannuation: many contractors invoice super separately or contribute personally. This tool does not deduct SG/super from take-home — treat super as an extra cashflow item.

FAQ

  • How do I convert an AU contractor day rate to annual income?

    Multiply your GST-exclusive day rate by billable days. Default is 220 days. Example: $800 x 220 = $176,000 a year before tax.

  • Which tax year and brackets does AU Freelancer Calc use?

    ATO resident rates for FY2025-26: 0% to $18,200, 16% to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000, then 45%. Medicare 2% with shade $28,011 to $35,013. Last checked 2026-08-22.

  • Does estimated take-home include GST, super, or HELP?

    No. Taxable income is GST-exclusive revenue. Super, HELP/HECS, MLS, offsets and deductions are not modelled. Estimate only.

  • Example: what does a $800 day rate come to after tax?

    Labelled example $800/day GST exclusive over 220 days: annual $176,000 (ex GST), income tax $46,458, Medicare $3,520, take-home $126,022.

  • Is this official ATO tax advice?

    No. Free estimate for AU freelancers, not tax advice. Confirm with the ATO or a registered tax agent.
